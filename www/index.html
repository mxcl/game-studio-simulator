The Dev

mxcl
Legendary open source dev with battle-tested history.

Max Howell knows how creative people and developers think. As the creator of Homebrew — used by tens of millions — and PromiseKit — built into over 100,000 apps — he's spent his career building tools that get out of the way and let people make things. He understands the itch: you want to create something, not lose weeks figuring out how to build the thing that lets you create it.

That's exactly the spirit behind Game Studio Simulator. GSS has been open source from day one, and it's staying that way. Max is building in public, with Twitch streams coming soon at twitch.tv/mxhwll — no smoke and mirrors, just real development in the open.

This is grassroots. Built for real people by real people — not finance bros or gatekeepers. Come be part of the journey. We can't wait to see what you make.
